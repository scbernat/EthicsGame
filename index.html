The Car Year Game

One year as a college student in our town. Every group gets a random budget and has to get to class and work. Survive all six rounds and keep the biggest share of your money.

1. Groups

2. Budget cards

Deal the cards at random, or match the envelopes each group drew. With 4 groups, card C is left out.

3. Phone voting (optional)

30

Final scores

Score = (cash + car value) ÷ year total. Highest percentage wins; ties go to fewer strikes.
